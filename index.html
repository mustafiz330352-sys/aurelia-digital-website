Questions we hear often

How long does a website take?

Most business websites take 2 to 4 weeks. Online stores and custom apps take longer. You get a clear timeline before we start.

Can I update the site myself?

Yes. We build on tools you can edit without coding and we train you to change text, images and products.

What do I need to provide?

Your logo if you have one, your text and photos, and examples of sites you like. If you have none of these, we can help create them.

Do you offer support after launch?

Yes. Every project includes free fixes for 30 days. After that you can choose a monthly support plan.

How does payment work?

A deposit to start, then the balance on delivery. Larger projects are split into milestones.
